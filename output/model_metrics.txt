Limitations
-----------
- Class imbalance (~12% spam): accuracy alone is misleading; precision/recall/F1 on spam are reported.
- Bag-of-words TF-IDF ignores word order and context; obfuscated spam (e.g. 'fr33') can evade it.
- The corpus is small and dated (UK/Singapore SMS, ~2000s-2012); performance on modern SMS may differ.
- A single test split of ~1000 messages (~130 spam): one error changes spam recall by ~0.8 points.
